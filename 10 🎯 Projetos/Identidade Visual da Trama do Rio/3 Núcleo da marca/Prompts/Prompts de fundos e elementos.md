# Prompts · fundos e elementos da Trama

Projeto: [[Identidade Visual da Trama do Rio]]. Paleta aprovada em 2026-10-20 (ver a página do núcleo). Servem para gerar **material de montagem** (fundos, formas, papel, selos) que depois vai para o Canva e para as peças do kit e dos posts. Não geram o post pronto.

## Qual ferramenta usar

| O que gerar | Comece por | Por quê |
|---|---|---|
| Elementos soltos (formas, doodles, papel, fita, selos) | **ChatGPT (imagem)** | Entrega PNG com fundo transparente de forma nativa. |
| Fundos de padrão e texturas | **Nano Banana (Gemini)** e compare com o ChatGPT | Aceita muitas imagens de referência e costuma manter melhor a paleta. |

Isso vem de testes de blogs independentes, não de benchmark oficial. Por isso a regra é: **na primeira folha de cada tipo, rode o mesmo prompt nos dois e fique com o que sair melhor.** Depois usa só esse.

## Como usar (5 passos)

1. Abra uma conversa nova. Anexe as referências **07, 08, 09 e 12** e um print da seção de paleta da página do núcleo. **Nunca** anexe fotos de pessoas.
2. Cole o **Bloco comum** e, na mesma mensagem, um dos prompts abaixo.
3. Peça 3 variações e, para os fundos, peça também **outras duas combinações de cor** (trocando só as cores).
4. Salve em `3 Núcleo da marca/Elementos/` com nome claro: `fundo-xadrez-chiclete.png`, `folha-formas-1.png`.
5. Me avise. Eu **separo cada elemento da folha** em arquivos individuais (por script), olho o resultado contra as referências e monto a página v2 com eles. Elementos que se tocam na folha ficam juntos, então peça espaço entre eles.

Se o fundo transparente não vier (aparece xadrez cinza e branco desenhado, ou fundo branco), peça de novo com "solid flat cream background #FFF4E0, no shadows" e tire o fundo no Canva Pro (Removedor de fundo), que funciona bem em formas chapadas.

Escrevi em inglês porque os geradores seguem melhor. Peça sempre **sem texto** na imagem: eles erram o português. O texto entra no Canva.

## Bloco comum (cole sempre primeiro)

```
Brand graphic assets for a Brazilian women's book club. Audience: adult women, 25 to 40. Attitude: warm, witty, confident and grown-up, never childish.

Attached references (07, 08, 09, 12): take their structure and spirit, bold flat color, patterns, layered paper elements, hand-cut shapes. Do not copy their content, characters or logos.

Palette: use ONLY these colors, with exact hex values. Bubblegum pink #F2609F (the main accent color, not the most frequent: backgrounds are usually calmer), cotton-candy pink #FFC2E2, guava coral #FF6B4A, peach #FFB59E, cream #FFF4E0, wine #5A1830 (for lines and small details, never black), lime #CFDD5A and periwinkle #8FA6F2 (small accents only, at most 10% of the area). All colors at a similar saturation: warm and sweet but firm. No pure white, no black, no other colors.

Rendering: flat solid color, matte, like screen-printed or cut paper, with at most a fine paper grain. No gradients, no drop shadows, no 3D, no glossy highlights, no glow.

No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no people, no faces.

Must NOT look like: a child's crayon, marker or pencil drawing, a teen magazine, generic clip-art or a stock vector pack, a supermarket flyer, a washed-out pastel palette, a cluttered sticker sheet. No hearts, no stars, no sparkles.
```

## A · Fundos de padrão

Todos: tile quadrado 1:1, **sem emendas** (repete sem corte), da borda à borda, sem moldura. Gere cada um em 3 combinações: (1) chiclete + algodão-doce, (2) goiaba + pêssego, (3) creme + pervinca.

**A1 · Xadrez**
```
Seamless repeating pattern tile, square 1:1, edge to edge. A gingham checkerboard of slightly imperfect squares, as if cut by hand from paper, so the squares are not perfectly even. Two colors only: bubblegum pink #F2609F and cotton-candy pink #FFC2E2. Fine paper grain. No border, no shadow, no text.
```

**A2 · Listra**
```
Seamless repeating pattern tile, square 1:1, edge to edge. Vertical stripes: wide bubblegum pink #F2609F stripes separated by thin cream #FFF4E0 lines, slightly wobbly and uneven as if printed by hand with a roller. Fine paper grain. No border, no shadow, no text.
```

**A3 · Bolinhas e confete**
```
Seamless repeating pattern tile, square 1:1, edge to edge. A guava coral #FF6B4A background scattered with round dots of different sizes in peach #FFB59E and cream #FFF4E0, plus a few tiny dots of lime #CFDD5A, irregular spacing, like confetti. Fine paper grain. No border, no shadow, no text.
```

**A4 · Formas orgânicas** (a ideia do quadro de cores do 06)
```
Seamless repeating pattern tile, square 1:1, edge to edge. A cream #FFF4E0 background with loosely scattered flat organic shapes: rounded flowers, drops, half-moons, blobs, short wavy lines and circles, in bubblegum pink, guava coral, peach, lime and periwinkle. Generous space between shapes, no overlaps, shapes are smooth, flat and without outlines. Fine paper grain. No text.
```

**A5 · Ondas**
```
Seamless repeating pattern tile, square 1:1, edge to edge. Wavy horizontal stripes, alternating bubblegum pink #F2609F and cotton-candy pink #FFC2E2, hand-cut look with slightly uneven waves. Fine paper grain. No border, no shadow, no text.
```

**A6 · Liso com grão** (para fundo "liso, nunca sem grão")
```
Seamless paper texture tile, square 1:1. A flat bubblegum pink #F2609F surface with a fine, even paper grain and a few tiny fibers, neutral flat lighting, no creases, no shadows, no pattern. Edge to edge.
```
Repita em creme `#FFF4E0` e em goiaba `#FF6B4A`.

**A7 · Quadriculado de caderno** (fundo do topo da página do núcleo)
```
Seamless repeating pattern tile, square 1:1, edge to edge. A very pale bluish white #F6F8FF background with a fine, delicate grid of thin lines like notebook graph paper, in periwinkle #8FA6F2 at low opacity, 1 pixel thin, evenly spaced. Subtle, calm and clean. Fine paper grain. No border, no shadow, no text.
```
Essa combinação é a única que usa o branquinho `#F6F8FF` como fundo.

## B · Folha de formas soltas
```
A sheet of 16 loose flat shapes on a transparent background, evenly spaced, none touching or overlapping, with generous space between them for easy cutting. Shapes: rounded flowers, drops, half-moons, blobs, wavy squiggles, circles and scalloped badges, in different sizes. Solid flat colors from the palette, no outlines, no gradient, no shadow. Mix of colors, with bubblegum pink and guava coral the most frequent.
```

## C · Folha de doodles (humor desenhado, estilo da referência 09)
```
A sheet of 14 hand-drawn doodle illustrations on a transparent background, evenly spaced, none touching. One single consistent family: the same medium line weight, even monoline, in wine #5A1830, with flat color fills from the palette placed slightly off the lines, like a risograph print with imperfect registration. Charming, a little imperfect, adult, like a designer's sketchbook, not a child's drawing. Subjects: an open book, a stack of books, a tea cup, reading glasses, a pencil, a bookmark, a flower, a potted plant, a candle, a cookie, a tote bag, an empty speech bubble, a pointing hand, a pair of peeking eyes. No crayon texture, no pencil shading, no cartoon faces on objects.
```

## D · Papel e fita (a camada do 12)

A sheet of photographic cut-out elements seen from above on a transparent background, evenly spaced, none touching. ```
Variações: papel algodão-doce `#FFC2E2` e papel pêssego `#FFB59E`.

## H · Rabiscos de caderno e scrapbook (uma cor só, para virar SVG)
Cole o **prompt**, troque `[LISTA]` por uma das três listas e gere uma folha de cada vez. A cor é preta de propósito: depois você vetoriza em casa e recolore com as cores da marca. Não use o bloco comum aqui, porque ele pede cor.
```
A sheet of hand-drawn scribble marks for a scrapbook, in ONE single flat color only: pure black (#000000) on a pure white background (#FFFFFF). Landscape 3:2, about 30 marks.

Style: loose, expressive and a little imperfect, like the margins of a teenager's notebook, a bullet journal or a scrapbook, drawn with a marker or a gel pen. Rounded line ends, slightly wobbly lines, confident quick strokes. Charming and adult, not childish, not corporate, not glossy. It must feel at home next to paper collage and mixed media.

Technical rules, very important: only black and white, no grey, no gradients, no shadows, no texture, no paper, no pencil grain, no soft or blurry edges. Crisp clean edges. One consistent medium-thick line weight across the whole sheet (never thinner than about 8 px on a 2000 px wide image) so every mark can be traced into a vector. Every mark is a single stroke or a solid shape, with no overlapping lines inside one mark. Every mark is isolated, at least 80 px of empty white space between marks, none touching or overlapping, in different sizes and at different angles.

No letters, no numbers, no words, no hearts, no photos, no borders around the sheet.

Marks to draw: [LISTA]
```

**Lista 1 · setas e sublinhados**
```
straight arrows, curved arrows, a looping arrow, a double-headed arrow, a zigzag arrow, an arrow with a curl at the tail, a small arrow pointing down-right; underlines: a wavy underline, a double underline, a thick brushy underline, a zigzag underline, a scribbled hatch underline; highlights: a loose open circle drawn around something, a hand-drawn bracket, a lopsided hand-drawn box with uneven corners, a swoosh stroke
```

**Lista 2 · brilhos, estrelas e bolinhas**
```
shine marks made of 3 to 5 short dashes radiating from a point, groups of short dashes, irregular 4-point sparkle stars, irregular 5-point stars (some outlined, some solid), small plus signs, small asterisks, rows of three dots, a loose cluster of confetti dots, a dotted line, a spiral, small crosses, tiny circles
```

**Lista 3 · carinhas e pequenos símbolos**
```
a simple smiley face, a winking smiley, a smiley with tongue out, a big grin face, a sleepy face with closed eyes, a checkmark, a lightning bolt, a small cloud outline, a little 5-petal flower made of line, a curved speech-bubble tail, a crown made of three zigzag points, an eye with lashes, a sparkle with a tail
```
**Depois de gerar:** mande a folha para mim, que eu separo cada marca em PNG (a cor única e o fundo branco ajudam). Em casa, vetorize (no Inkscape: Caminho > Vetorizar bitmap) e salve como SVG. Se uma folha sair com cinza, sombra ou linha fina, peça de novo "pure black and white only, thicker line".

## I · Alfabeto de recortes de revista (para montar palavras na colagem)
Gere poucas letras por folha, para cada recorte sair grande e nítido: **20 por folha**, em landscape. Troque `[LETRAS]` e repita até completar (A a J, K a T, U a Z mais ! ? &, 0 a 9). A imagem de referência que você mandou tinha só cerca de 70 px por letra, pouco para uso em post. Aqui o objetivo é cerca de 300 px por letra.
```
A sheet of exactly 20 cut-out paper letters for a ransom-note style collage, on a pure white background (#FFFFFF). Landscape 3:2, as high resolution as possible. The letters to draw, one piece each, in this order: [LETRAS].

Each letter is printed in a different typeface, as if cut from different magazines: heavy slab serif, high-contrast serif, italic script, chunky sans, condensed, rounded. Each letter sits on its own irregular scissor-cut piece of colored paper, with a slightly uneven cut edge and a faint paper grain. The letter must be perfectly legible and correctly shaped.

Colors (use only these): paper #FDFBF4, cream #FFF4E0, cotton candy #FFC2E2, peach #FFB59E, guava coral #FF6B4A, bubblegum pink #F2609F, cherry #C63236, wine #5A1830, lime #CFDD5A, periwinkle #8FA6F2. Make each piece a different combination of paper color and letter color, with good contrast between them. Pink and coral appear most, lime and periwinkle only in small doses.

Technical rules: every piece is isolated, with at least 60 px of empty white space around it, none touching or overlapping. All pieces about the same size, each letter large, filling its piece. No shadows, no 3D, no background pattern, no extra decorations, no text other than the letters. Original design, not a copy of any existing sticker sheet.
```
**Depois de gerar:** mande a folha para mim, que eu separo cada recorte, nomeio (`alfabeto-a-rosa`, `alfabeto-a-lima`) e organizo na aba Alfabeto do site.

## O que eu faço com o resultado
- Separo cada elemento das folhas em arquivos individuais.
- Comparo contra as referências 07, 08, 09 e 12 e digo o que pegou e o que não pegou.
- Monto a página v2 do núcleo com os fundos e elementos reais, e as amostras do kit usando eles.
- Se algo sair com cara de giz de cera, de adesivo genérico ou de revista teen, ajusto o prompt antes de você gerar mais.
